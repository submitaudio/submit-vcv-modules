## v2.18.0

- Updated the rotary controls to the current Submit Audio design and aligned them accurately with the panel scales.
- Rebuilt the DSP around a high-quality SSL 4K-style four-band channel-EQ topology.
- Added a musical 18 dB/octave high-pass filter from 16–350 Hz and a 12 dB/octave low-pass filter from 22–3 kHz, both with an Off position.
- Tuned the four fixed EQ bands to 80 Hz, 400 Hz, 2.5 kHz and 10 kHz with a ±15 dB range.
- Added proportional-Q behaviour to both mid bands for broader subtle shaping and tighter strong boosts or cuts.
- Moved the stereo filters to double-precision processing and added smoothed controls and click-free filter engagement.
- Preserved the existing parameters, ports, defaults and patch structure.
